# WrymWretch: deep dive

Everything proposed here is an addition unless marked as the source's; section 6 lists each one
for approval. Quotations are verbatim
from `creative-writing/vault/02_Novels/02_WrymWretch.md` (WW, with line) and
`creative-writing/vault/05_Worldbuilding/03_World_Codex_of_Wyrmreach.md` (Codex, with line).

## 1. Where it stands

**What works.** The first five minutes are the best in the collection: a map label losing its
letters while you stand on the place it names. The core rule (recording protects one thing, so
choose what to keep) is a real decision and it reaches every system. The five 1D games are tied to
images the author wrote. Each dragon flying differently suits six short prologues, and the Act III
contract keeps the added act removable.

**What is weak.**

1. **Nobody in the party wants anything from anybody else.** After the six pairs meet at minute 74
   they are a roster. No pair hides anything, disagrees, or is changed by another. The protagonist
   of the second half is a meter.
2. **The middle has an attack, not a reversal.** Nothing learned at minute 100 overturns what was
   believed at minute 20: remember more, lose less. The Unwriter is a creed with 260 Life.
3. **The verb is never questioned.** Remembering is always right and always +2. Retelling at the
   fire turns listeners into witnesses, whom the Remnants kill, yet no listener is asked. A game
   about memory with no case for forgetting has no argument.
4. **The later tiers turn dials.** Concentration II and III are more cards and a shorter interval;
   Glyph III is a faster slip. The web build plays one scene of each mode, and one dragon of six.
   Links between layers are plus and minus on a meter.
5. **Remembered Whole costs nothing the player chose.** The Architect Rises is a fail state. The
   final choice is three buttons pressed after the meters have decided.

In art: every still is a place or a prop. Twelve protagonists have no faces.

## 2. The story, developed

### The question

**Dramatic question:** when a world is held together by remembering, who is made to carry the
memory, and did anyone ask them?

The party **wants** everything back. It **needs** to learn that a memory always has a carrier, and
that its ancestors once chose the carrier for it.

### The secret under the story

Aeris asks whether the sickness is her fault, and Aetherius answers: "Because of us, Aetherius corrected gently. Our bond was dormant
too long." (WW 149). The prison is "sealed by memory itself" (Codex 30), and only now do "Dragons
awaken from dormancy" (Codex 30). Nothing says why the dragons slept.

**The reading I propose (addition): the dormancy was the price.** The six tribes sealed Izladar with
a memory large enough to make a wall: their memory of their own dragons. Ilvarra's existing line, "They bound him with our
names", already says it. So the dragons waking is not the cure. It is the coin coming back because
the wall is coming down. Every bonding scene the player loved in the first hour was a symptom.

A second seed: the prophecy reads "When the last memory fades, the Astral
Architect shall rise." (WW 77), and the thing in the chasm is "a voice half‑formed, a memory
refusing to die" (WW 260) that says "I… remember… me…" (WW 101). **The last memory is his own.** The
scholar is the final witness to himself, and his remembering is the last nail in his own prison.
The Remnants believe they are freeing him. They are erasing him; what rises is what is left when
the scholar forgets he was one. This adds no biography and gives him no choice.

### The opposing force

**The Unwriter wants no one to wake as half of something again.** He was Wyrmguard, Ilvarra's
wingmate, and the name inside his mask is **Dorrin Vael**. When the dragons woke, his did not: Tarn
died in the long sleep, and Dorrin woke remembering a whole soul and holding half. To him the
tribes' bargain was a theft and memory is the wound. He is wrong about what will rise, and the
player can prove it to him.

### The endings, and what each now costs

All remain proposals; the source has no ending.

- **The Rememberer.** The pair that stays does knowingly what the tribes did to their dragons
  blind. If Seraphyne keeps watch, the last shot is the sealed page in her lap, unopened.
- **Remembered Whole.** New cost. A world that holds the seal by remembering can never let anything
  fade. Grief does not soften in the six realms. Dorrin was half right.
- **Dormant Again.** The ancestral bargain, chosen with open eyes. If the fourth row was read,
  each elf leaves one line for whoever wakes next.
- **The Architect Rises.** If scene 12 was reached, the voice loses its last word first: "I…
  remember…". What steps through does not know it was a scholar.
- **NEW, The Empty Saddle** (proposal). Dorrin, reminded of his own name and Tarn's, takes the
  watch. He has no one to go home to. Conditions: a fourth answer at the statues, open only with
  `unwriter_reminded`, Tarn's rib tuned, and the fourth row read. Cost: the man who killed the
  witnesses keeps the world's memory, and he is mortal. The debt is passed forward again. Last
  image: the Record's final page, headed "Who next?"

## 3. Obstacles and puzzles

### Each mode: teach, test, twist

**Twin flight.** *Teach:* the sail tears; catch Aeris. *Test:* Umbra blinks shadow to shadow.
*Twist:* formation, where the lead decides what everyone survives.
New: **Wake** (a dragon's ink trail lasts 2 s; a pair flying inside it ignores gusts, so order is
shelter). **Unpainted silk** (where a realm is under 40 the scroll is blank: hazards are still
there, drawn only for recorded places). **Borrowed gift** (the second pair fires its gift through the
lead's: Sylvan grows a perch on Gaia's raised stone).
*Set-piece, Ghost-fire descent:* the chasm is black. "A ripple of light tore across the chasm,
illuminating the cliffs in ghostly fire." (WW 99). Each pulse of the voice shows a band for two
seconds; then you fly it from memory.

**Forgetting run.** *Teach:* Recall an outline. *Test:* swim up as the blank rises. *Twist:* someone
else has to cross what you remember.
New: **False outlines** (drawn with no shadow; Recalling one burns Focus and holds nothing).
**Carried lantern** (lift a touchstone and plant it as your checkpoint; carrying slows you 15%).
**Turn and face** (hold Recall backward: 40 Focus re-inks 6 m of erased ground).
*Set-piece, the bone bridge:* halfway over, Ilvarra stops. The curse has taken why she is running.
She is drawn in outline. You Recall a person.

**Star Chart.** *Teach:* fragments as constraints. *Test:* false stars. *Twist:* undo a figure to
find the one beneath.
New: **Shared stars** (the Stag's antler tip is the Harp's peg; one line must satisfy both).
**The lying strip** (one fragment per plate is in a Remnant hand; a figure named to it gilds black
and must be unpicked).
*Set-piece, plate III:* each line of the Sigil you lift returns a syllable to the voice. The man
beneath is one star short. It is the star Seraphyne placed.

**The Slipping Glyph.** *Teach:* find what stays. *Test:* the kill-list, on the camp's clock.
*Twist:* the stable part moves.
New: **Paired glyphs** (two that hold only while both are in the circle; widen the circle and it
dims). **Shy glyphs** (stable only when unwatched; read them at the rim of the circle).
**World-held parts** (see the long puzzle).
*Set-piece, the seal:* the sixth row is the name, and it is the first glyph in the game that does
not slip. Someone has been holding it still for millennia.

**Concentration.** *Teach:* faces blank. *Test:* choose the blank from memory. *Twist:* the game
starts lying.
New: **False faces** (a Remnant card shows a face that has no partner; pairing it locks a lie into
the Record until unpicked). **Triples** (Ilvarra's memories need face, name and sound).
*Set-piece:* Cendre's face is clear glass before the first flip. The only record is what Ilvarra
said at the fire: one horn shorter than the other. You pick it from eight blank panes by its lead
line.

**Six Realms.** *Teach:* the Syndicate. *Test:* four house rules. *Twist:* the board loses its own
goal.
New: **The blank tessera** (a neutral Remnant piece enters with the first tide and moves one hex
toward the nearest Threshold each round; pieces beside it lose their powers). **The worn seventh
colour** (Ilvarra's board has a seventh piece, the Wyrmguard, that moves as whatever it last stood
beside).
*Set-piece:* against the Tidecallers the tide takes the centre. Wrymreach goes void, and the match
must be won by Recall alone.

**Heartstone.** *Teach:* still is right. *Test:* Fuse two shards. *Twist:* the right chord needs
something missing.
New: **Sympathy** (a strike also cracks tray shards one step from a placed pitch, so tray order
matters). **Written shards** (some carry a Record line; shatter one and the line goes). **The
rest** (one slot must stay empty).
*Set-piece, Tarn's rib:* the choir is tuned, the door opens, and one rib still rings dull. Tune it
anyway, to no purpose, and the hall plays a seventh note.

### Three links between layers

1. **Names become ground.** Each ward patient kept in Concentration I is a lit lantern in runs III
   and IV, with that patient's name on it. Lose three and those stretches are dark.
2. **Stars become the route.** Each constellation gilded lights its realm's band permanently in the
   Ghost-fire descent. A black-gilded figure lights a false band.
3. **The Heartstone's memory changes rooms.** Keep the founding: Stonekeeper home hexes cannot be
   tide-taken. Keep the vision: the Echo's intents are shown. Keep the song: the Bone Choir starts
   with one rib tuned, and it is audible in the 3D hall before the page opens.

### The long puzzle: the fourth row

The monolith's fourth row has no stable part on the page, because its stable parts are held
elsewhere, one per realm, each in plain sight in a 3D scene: the crack in the split tree, a rune on
Aetherius's scales (WW 139), a vein in the Heartstone, a kelp line on the seabed, a candle shadow
in the forbidden chamber, a star in the Priory dome. Noticed and recorded, each fills one part.
At the seal, with all six, the row reads as the terms of the bargain: what was given, by whom.
Reading it opens the fourth answer at the statues and changes the text of the other three.

### A second playthrough

The player chooses the order of the prologues, which changes what the first seal drains.
Seraphyne's early naming of the sigil and every "Remember me." (WW 49) read differently once the
price is known. Five keeper variants remain, and The Empty Saddle needs a route most first runs
miss.

## 4. Art plan

**Style bible.**

- **Palette:** bone `#E9E2D0`, tarnished silver `#8E97A3`, void `#0B0D1A`, crack ink `#17151A`; one
  accent per realm: moss `#7FA650`, sky `#A9C7E8`, molten `#E0762E`, sea `#2F7FA8`, violet
  `#6B3FA0`, star gold `#E7C566`. Forgetting is paper `#F1E8D6`.
- **Line:** carved, not drawn: every edge a ridge with a rim light. Pages keep each mode's own style.
- **Texture:** ivory grain, silver repoussé, fine black cracks.
- **Lighting:** one cold rim, one accent source, a starry void behind. Light dims with the realm.
- **Never shown:** Izladar's face or body; the Architect except as a pressure on the veil; the
  dying sage; any wound in close view; a Remnant's face until its name is returned.

## 5. Roadmap

**Phase 1, most effect for least work (text and flags).** The Price at Ilvarra's fire; Ysolde's
request and Burn as mercy; Dorrin's name on the kill-list's fifth row; the new costs in the four
endings; the three layer links; pair portraits and title lettering. None needs a new scene.

**Phase 2, new play.** The fourth row and its six world-held parts; The Second Reader inside Star
Chart II; Tarn's rib inside Heartstone II; the Ghost-fire descent; the bone-bridge outline beat;
one new element per mode in the web build, starting with false outlines and false faces.

**Phase 3, the rest.** The Empty Saddle ending and its card; the five unbuilt flight models;
selectable prologue order; the remaining new elements; chapter cards and ornaments.

**Needs the author's decision before work starts:**

- The dormancy as the price of the seal (it rereads WW 149 and Codex 30).
- The voice as the seal's last witness (it extends answer 1 in DESIGN.md section 20).
- Seraphyne having read the forbidden page (it rests on one clause, WW 85).
- A fifth ending, and the new cost of Remembered Whole.
- Whether Dorrin, Tarn and Ysolde keep these names.
